# The note

On 2026-11-25 Coco posted a holiday note to the Fellowship of the Dome from the EcoDome LinkedIn profile [cocolata-ecodome](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cocolata-ecodome/). The filed copy is the [source summary](../sources/coco-linkedin-holiday.md). It is Coco's wording. It is an outreach note, not a signed program or a sponsorship.[^holiday-message]

Coco describes EcoDome as jamming in Charlottesville and now nationally, and as working toward a 50-foot pop-up dome in Charlottesville for Tom Tom 2027, with Hackadome leading into the festival. The idea stated is to build something together, then invite neighbors to step inside and try it. The [April 2027 pilot](../pilot/april-2027.md) already proposes a temporary 50-foot showcase with Tom Tom. This note uses "pop-up" and adds the name Hackadome. It does not give Hackadome's dates or format.

The presentation and Discord links in the original were not supplied with URLs.

# Asks

- If someone asks what EcoDome is, show the presentation and ask what they would love to experience in the dome.
- Ideas: one sentence in Discord, in the form "Inside the dome, I'd love to…"
- Makers: say in Discord what they like to build, film, code, teach, or dream up. Half-formed ideas are welcome.
- Sponsors: send Coco a name or make an introduction. "DM me" in the note refers to Coco. The note says the project is looking for local and national sponsors to cover the build, the tech, and the people so admission can be pay-what-you-can. The same aim is already on the [budget page](../pilot/budget-and-sponsorship.md) from the November 24 Discord note. This message repeats it as a public ask. It is not a signed sponsorship.

How someone adds material to the catalog, which is a different path, is on [contribution and catalog](contribution.md).
