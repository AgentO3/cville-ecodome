# Structure-only build times

The [feasibility research](../sources/feasibility-research.md) quotes other operators. It says the 12 to 14 hour figures are for the structure only, with experienced crews.[^feasibility]

| Source named in the study | Build time for a 50-foot dome | Crew |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pacific Domes spec sheet | About 14 hours | 4 |
| DomeGuys project write-up | About 12 to 14 hours | 5 |
| Lumen & Forge, large projection domes, 50 to 120 feet | About 20 to 50 hours | Not stated |
| SoFlo Studio | Plan site access 3 to 4 days before and after the event | Not stated |

Adding a liner, projector rigging, warp and blend, audio, HVAC, flooring, and egress lighting, the study's realistic plan for a projection event is **2 to 3 days of install and 1 to 2 days of strike**. It says DomeGuys' own crews build in about half the time of inexperienced crews, so the first builds are slower. Pacific Domes lists setup and strike for a 50-foot dome at $16,500, four staff and a 10,000-pound reach forklift, excluding shipping, equipment, and travel.

# Roles and site needs

Roles the study lists: a crew chief on every install; three or four general builders; an equipment operator; a projection technician, called the scarcest skill; a show operator; front-of-house staff on ticketed days; a sales and event manager; and a contract content producer. It says install crews must be on payroll or covered for workers' compensation. The insurance consequence is on the [insurance page](../regulations/insurance.md).

Every booking, it says, needs level ground or a hard surface, an anchoring method matched to that surface, utilities marked before staking, power for projectors, audio, HVAC, and the liner fan, fire access and exit paths, and a written weather rule. Central Virginia summers make cooling essential inside a dark dome, and winters raise heat, snow, and ice. The strongest outdoor months are described as spring and fall, which is also peak wedding and festival season. An indoor placement needs ceiling height above about 25 feet plus rigging clearance.

The capital gear for this work is estimated on [projection and AV](../dome/projection-and-av.md). Permit conditions are on [Virginia permits](../regulations/virginia-permits.md).
