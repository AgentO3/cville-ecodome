# Cville EcoDome / EcoDome Charlottesville — Public Web Research

**Research date:** Monday, November 23, 2026 (America/New_York)  
**Scope:** Public web only (official sites, news, event listings, business filings, social accounts, and venue pages). Facts below are tied to sources. Claims that appear only in founder-authored materials are labeled as self-reported. No facts were invented to fill gaps.

**Bottom line:** EcoDome Charlottesville (often styled ECODOME or ECODOME CVILLE) is a **proposed, not yet built** 50-foot LED-panel immersive “edutainment” venue for about 250 people, led by founder **Coco Hunter**. It is incorporated as a Virginia LLC (February 2025), has a public website and social accounts, has held community events around the IX Art Park / Three Notch’d campus, and was presented at the November 18, 2026 Cville AI Explorers meetup. A conceptual digital twin places a hemisphere on the IX Art Park upper lot. **No city or county permit, no independent confirmation of a locked site, no IRS 501(c)(3) record, and no C-Ville Weekly / Daily Progress / Cardinal News / Cville Right Now coverage were found.**

---

## Summary

EcoDome is a Charlottesville project to build a multipurpose immersive venue centered on a **50-foot LED-panel dome** with a stated capacity of **250 people**. The official site describes programming across immersive art and entertainment, workshops, youth education, community events, indoor–outdoor activation, and a “future-ready innovation hub,” with a sustainability and digital-literacy framing ([ecodomecville.com](https://ecodomecville.com/)).

The project is still in the vision, fundraising, and site-discussion stage. Founder materials say the team is “speaking with location partners,” running a membership campaign, and has “strong support from the City of Charlottesville” and a “prime downtown location” ([CanvasRebel interview, April 10, 2025](https://canvasrebel.com/meet-coco-hunter/)). Those city-support and downtown-site claims were **not corroborated** on city websites, council agendas, or permit databases searched for this report.

Community activity is documented:

- A March 9, 2025 Eventbrite “Metaverse & Site Unveiling,” relocated to **Three Notch’d at IX** (520 2nd Street SE), advertised as a chance to hear “where ECODOME will rise” — not as a completed venue opening ([Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/ecodome-exclusive-new-location-at-three-notchd-tickets-1245565553099)).
- A May 3, 2025 potluck in Afton that promised EcoDome updates ([Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/potluck-party-magic-celebrating-cocos-birthday-tickets-1333255746829)).
- April 14, 2026 **Cville Great Mother Fest** at IX Art Park, organized by Coco Hunter and covered by NBC29 and CBS19; CBS19 identified her as “Founder of ECODOME Charlottesville.”
- November 18, 2026 **Cville AI Explorers** Show & Share at Studio IX (969 2nd St SE), where the public Meetup listing named **only Coco Hunter** as the EcoDome speaker ([Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/cville-tech/events/317209160/); also listed by [UVA Engineering](https://engineering.virginia.edu/news-events/events/cville-ai-explorers-cvaiex)).

A public 3D experience at [eco-dome-ix-art-park.netlify.app](https://eco-dome-ix-art-park.netlify.app/) presents “the Eco Dome at IX Art Park,” credits **The Futurist Network**, and states that the placement in the IX Art Park **upper lot is a concept placement** whose exact geographic anchor was not saved in the source. Scene snapshot dated **25 September 2026**. Kahlil Calavas is publicly CTO / creative technologist at TerraLux, the company behind The Futurist Network; the twin site itself does not print his name.

**Tina** (portfolio: [justbeing.space/Portfolio](https://www.justbeing.space/Portfolio)) is an immersive installation artist with work in Virginia, but **no public web page found in this research links her by name to EcoDome**. The November 2026 Meetup speaker list does not include her.

Local news coverage of the *dome project itself* is thin. The only mainstream local TV identification of EcoDome found is the CBS19 Great Mother March story (April 14, 2026). CanvasRebel published a founder interview in April 2025. No matching articles were found on C-Ville Weekly, The Daily Progress, Cardinal News, or Cville Right Now.

## Venue / site

**No operating dome exists in Charlottesville under this name.** The official site’s framing is “COMING TO CVILLE,” and it does not name a street address or parcel.

What *is* documented:

1. **Official language is downtown / central, not a locked address.** The site calls it a “centrally located destination.” CanvasRebel (April 2025) says the team is “actively speaking with location partners,” with a hoped-for “prime downtown location,” and claims City of Charlottesville support. **No matching city press release, grant award, council minute, or permit was found.**

2. **IX Art Park is a repeated activity and concept site, not a proven construction site.**
   - March 9, 2025 unveiling was held at Three Notch’d, 520 2nd Street SE, described as “Three Notch’d at IX,” after a relocation “due to unforeseen circumstances.” The copy still spoke of learning “where ECODOME will rise,” which implies the brewery was the *meeting* place, not necessarily the *build* place.
   - April 14, 2026 Great Mother Fest was at IX Art Park.
   - November 18, 2026 EcoDome talk was at Studio IX, 969 2nd St SE (same neighborhood / IX campus).
   - The Futurist Network digital twin explicitly says: “The Eco Dome is a **concept placement in the upper lot**; its exact geographic anchor was not saved in the source. Position and heading can be adjusted and exported.”

3. **IX Art Park / Looking Glass are existing immersive arts venues**, but they are a walk-through museum and outdoor park, not a 50-foot video dome. They should not be conflated with EcoDome.

4. **Planning and permits.** Searches of public web indexes for Charlottesville or Albemarle site plans, special-use permits, or building permits using EcoDome / LED dome / related personal names returned **no matches**. Absence from search is not proof no filing exists, only that none was found on the public web in this pass.

## Design & technology

**Confirmed from the official site** ([ecodomecville.com](https://ecodomecville.com/)):

- 50-foot **LED-panel** dome
- Designed to fit **250 people**
- Described as a multipurpose edutainment venue for immersive experiences and entertainment, creative workshops, education and youth programming, community and cultural events, indoor–outdoor activation, and a “future-ready innovation hub”
- Vision language: digital innovation, sustainable design, eco-conscious practices, digital literacy

**Self-reported or secondary (not independently verified):**

- CanvasRebel: “fully immersive, multi-purpose creative space”; “environmentally conscious model, showcasing renewable energy, sustainable materials, and community-driven design”; wellness and inclusivity as core values.
- LinkedIn (founder): “$7M+ eco-conscious venue”; “tech-enabled arts and education hub integrating immersive media, interactive technologies, and sustainability”; STEAM education collaborations.
- A September 2025 LinkedIn share by Coco Hunter about Arizona Science Center’s **Dorrance DOME** (a 60-foot Cosm CX System LED dome) is an *example of what is possible*, not a statement that EcoDome has purchased Cosm technology ([LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cocolata-ecodome_new-identity-reflects-cutting-edge-led-technology-activity-7363320689564524545-brQX)).
- Alignable listing mentions VR expeditions, “cosmic disco dances,” and mindfulness soundbaths as offered experiences; that is marketing copy for a venue that is not yet built ([Alignable](https://www.alignable.com/charlottesville-va/ecodome-cville)).

**Digital twin / visualization** ([eco-dome-ix-art-park.netlify.app](https://eco-dome-ix-art-park.netlify.app/), fetched Nov 23, 2026):

- “A living landscape. A dome of moving light. Step into the Eco Dome at IX Art Park.”
- Credits: 8 original models, 14 saved viewpoints, **180° hemispherical dome**
- “The video dome uses the source’s **8 m radius**, 360° horizontal coverage, and 90° vertical arc.” An 8 m radius is a **16 m (~52.5 ft) diameter**, slightly larger than the official 50 ft (15.24 m) figure. The report does not try to reconcile this; both figures are recorded as published.
- Satellite view reuses the IX Art Park digital twin’s geographic reference; placement in the upper lot is conceptual.
- Scene snapshot: 25 September 2026. Source media retains original ownership. Satellite imagery: Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics, GIS User Community.

**Promo / virtual-tour videos** on the official YouTube channel [youtube.com/@ECODOMECville](https://www.youtube.com/@ECODOMECville) (channel ID `UCI96bKfsK0PYEoTee8Gzr0w`):
