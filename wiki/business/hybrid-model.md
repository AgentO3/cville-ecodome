# What the study proposes

The [November 2026 feasibility research](../sources/feasibility-research.md), provided by the project team, sketches a hybrid business for central Virginia: mobile 50-foot dome and 360-degree projection rentals, plus seasonal ticketed shows. It is a draft for discussion. It is not a decision to replace the [April 2027 pilot](../pilot/april-2027.md) or the public site's venue language.[^feasibility]

The study's main investment claim is that the shell is the smaller cost. A frame and cover are described as available to purchase, without a projection liner or AV equipment. The multimedia layer is estimated at more than the dome itself, about $100,000 to $250,000 or more depending on tier. Details are on [dome hardware](../dome/hardware.md) and [projection and AV](../dome/projection-and-av.md).

# The tension it names

Every week the dome is installed as a ticketed venue is a week it cannot be rented. A structure standing 180 days or more stops being a temporary tent under Virginia rules and must meet the permanent building code. That line is on the [permits page](../regulations/virginia-permits.md). The study calls a seasonal run, for example spring and fall weekends at a host site, with mobile rentals in between, the most workable version.

It also says it found no local operator of large geodesic event or projection domes in central Virginia. National touring companies are the direct competitors, and local theaters, immersive-art shows, and tent companies are the indirect ones. See [competition](../competition/central-virginia.md).

# What would have to happen first

The study lists steps before buying the available dome: identify manufacturer, model, year, engineering and wind report, and flame certificate; meet the home-base building official and fire marshal; get a projection quote before committing; get insurance quotes from a broker who writes tent and event-rental programs; and test demand with letters of intent from a few anchor clients. Those items are also on [open questions](../questions/open-questions.md).

The [illustrative profit model](financial-model.md) says profit is plausible and highly sensitive to bookings. Suggested prices are on the [pricing page](pricing.md). Crew and install time are on [logistics](../operations/logistics-and-crew.md).
