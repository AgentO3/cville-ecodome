# Goal

The [AI Explorers deck](../sources/ai-explorers-deck.md) prints a fundraising goal of $175,000 for Charlottesville's 2027 pilot. The slide says the pilot's scope, location, programming, and partnership terms remain subject to approval. The allocation slide says the figures are a proposed allocation and that final costs, scope, and category boundaries remain under review.[^explorers-deck]

The six approximate lines sum to that goal. The sum is the [pilot budget computation](../computations/pilot-budget.md), not a second source of the numbers.

| Line | Approximate amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Dome and engineering | $70,000 |
| Pilot technology | $57,000 |
| People, programs, and crew | $33,000 |
| Freight and build | $8,000 |
| Power | $5,000 |
| Insurance and permits | $2,000 |
| Total planning allocation | $175,000 |

What the goal is described as buying: a dome to gather in; art, technology, and discovery; and access.

# Admission

Proposed admission is free and pay-what-you-can. A sponsorship focus area, "community access," is described as helping make admission affordable. The deck does not set ticket prices or a revenue target.

On November 24, Cocolata8 described a combination approach in Discord #general: ideally sponsors cover the full pilot cost, including build, tech, and people, so admission can stay pay-what-you-can; a mix of local sponsors and national tech sponsors would support the hackathon; BeCamp was named as a possible conversation; and an aligned lead sponsor naming the dome for the event was left open. None of that is a signed sponsorship. The paraphrase is the [Discord note](../sources/discord-general-2026-11-23-24.md).[^discord-general]

# Sponsorship

Focus areas are proposed, not fixed tiers. Packages and allocations depend on the final budget and partner agreements. Support can be cash, equipment, or services, by agreement. The five areas are: raise the dome; technology; sustainability; arts and programs; community access.

Proposed benefits, which the deck says depend on partner agreements, site approval, and capacity:

| Kind | What the slide offers |
| --- | --- |
| Recognition | Name or logo on the in-dome thank-you screen. Festival credit and press opportunities, where agreed. Press coverage is not guaranteed. |
| Presence | Dome signage and invitations to dome events. A role in the experience tied to the sponsor's focus area, by agreement. |
| Priority access | Proposed reserved seats, early registration, and guest passes. Limits and availability would be agreed so public access is protected. |

The deck invites a sponsor conversation with the EcoDome team and, on the path slide, with Coco. The [values page](../community/values.md) records a separate community preference about who funds the project. This page does not treat the preference and the sponsorship ask as the same decision.

The showcase these figures belong to is the [April 2027 pilot](april-2027.md).

# Other public asks

The $175,000 allocation is the deck's proposal. Other public pages use different words and do not cite that total.

- The [official site](../sources/official-site.md) invites founding members and donations. It does not mention April 2027 or $175,000.[^official-site]
- The April 10, 2025 interview describes a community membership campaign, and describes it as backed by the City of Charlottesville. No amount is stated.[^interview]
- The [Alignable listing](../sources/alignable-listing.md) lists founding circle memberships, investment and membership opportunities, and corporate sponsorships. No amounts are stated.[^alignable]

The insurance-and-permits line above is a planning number. No source records a permit application or a paid fee. The [values page](../community/values.md) is where the community's independence preference is kept next to these asks.

# A separate feasibility range

The [November 2026 feasibility study](../sources/feasibility-research.md) does not use $175,000. Its planning estimates, which it says are not quotes, put a multimedia layer at about $115,000 on an entry tier and about $275,000 or more on a professional tier, and starting capital for a professional launch at $250,000 to $400,000. A first-year insurance package is estimated at $12,000 to $25,000, against about $2,000 for insurance and permits together on the deck. The study is about buying and operating a projection dome. The deck is about a five-day pilot. Both stay on the record. On 2026-11-25, Owen decided that version 1 is projector-based, so the study's projection estimates are the planning path for that version. The decision does not adopt those dollar figures, and it does not replace the deck's $175,000 goal. LED remains a possible later upgrade. The estimates are unpacked on the [financial model](../business/financial-model.md) and the [projection page](../dome/projection-and-av.md).[^feasibility][^v1-projector]
