# Direct competitors

The [feasibility research](../sources/feasibility-research.md) says it found no company based in central Virginia renting large geodesic event or projection domes. The providers it lists travel to clients.[^feasibility]

| Company | Base in the study | Offer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DomeGuys International | Central Point, Oregon | Event and projection dome rentals and sales, 16 to 120 feet; a 50-foot standard dome in stock |
| Pacific Domes | Ashland, Oregon | Event and projection domes for sale and rent; a published price list |
| The Dome House | Ashland, Oregon | 20 to 90 foot event domes, projection options |
| NewMedia | New Jersey, with a Madrid office | Turnkey immersive domes with 360-degree projection |
| Lumen & Forge | Las Vegas | Geodesic and inflatable projection domes |
| SoFlo Studio | Miami | Event dome rentals with projection-mapping add-ons |

The study's claimed advantage for a local operator is no cross-country freight, faster site visits, lower crew travel, and local permit relationships. Pacific Domes also appears on the [geometry page](../dome/geometry.md) as the brand on the pilot deck's proposed package. That is not a statement that EcoDome has hired them. The [50-foot brochure](../sources/pacific-domes-50ft-brochure.md) retrieved 2026-11-25 describes the product and does not print a sale price or a rental rate. The price list named in the table remains the study's citation.[^pacific-brochure]

# Indirect competitors

| Name | What the study says |
| --- | --- |
| Science Museum of Virginia, Richmond | Private dome-theater rental, 250 seated, from about $2,500 with an operator. A low price anchor for watching something under a dome. A mobile dome has to sell going to the client's site |
| Exhibition Hub, Henrico | Rotating 360-degree immersive art shows in a space of about 20,000 square feet |
| Skyline Tent Company and Virginia Tent Rental, Charlottesville | Established tent rentals. Competitors for wedding and corporate budgets, and possible partners for flooring, HVAC, power, and labor |
| JMU John C. Wells Planetarium and the UVA Rotunda Planetarium | Free or low-cost public shows. They limit pricing for pure astronomy and are possible education partners |
| WHRO Environmental Dome, Hampton Roads | A 14-foot inflatable learning dome used by school divisions. The school market, the study says, is dominated by small inflatables |

Segments the study says are worth testing, not bookings: weddings at wineries, corporate events, university events, festivals, school field trips, and seasonal public shows such as sound baths, yoga, astronomy nights, and holiday shows. Prices proposed against this set are on the [pricing page](../business/pricing.md).
