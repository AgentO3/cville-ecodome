# What the sources say

The November 2026 [community notes](/sources/community-context.md) associate EcoDome with IX Art Park in Charlottesville, Virginia. They also point at a [digital twin](/prototypes/ix-art-park-twin.md) whose URL and description say it places the dome with satellite imagery and 360-degree views.[^community-context]

The [AI Explorers deck](/sources/ai-explorers-deck.md) does not name IX Art Park. Its path to April starts with "confirm site and delivery responsibilities," and "site access and approvals" is one of the items that unlock the pilot. The structure slide says the venue and festival dates are proposed, and that site and technical configuration are to be confirmed.[^explorers-deck]

Concept renderings in the deck show an outdoor installation. They are labeled as concept visualizations, or as a photograph of a dome with simulated projection. This wiki does not treat those pictures as proof of a chosen site.

# Public web, November 23, 2026

The [research pass](/sources/web-research.md) treats IX Art Park as a repeated activity and concept site, not as a proven construction site. **Confirmed:** the March 9, 2025 unveiling was at Three Notch'd, 520 2nd Street SE, described as Three Notch'd at IX, after a relocation. The April 14, 2026 Great Mother Fest was at IX Art Park. The November 18, 2026 talk was at Studio IX, 969 2nd St SE. Those listings are on the [events page](/events/documented-gatherings.md).[^web-research]

The [digital twin](/prototypes/ix-art-park-twin.md) says the Eco Dome is a concept placement in the upper lot and that its exact geographic anchor was not saved. IX Arts Foundation pages fetched for the report do not mention EcoDome. The Looking Glass is a walk-through museum on the same campus, not this 50-foot dome. No Charlottesville or Albemarle permit match was found. That absence is not proof that no filing exists.

Existing domes a person can actually visit are on [comparable domes](/comparables/nearby-domes.md).
