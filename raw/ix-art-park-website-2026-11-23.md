# IX Art Park website

Accessed 2026-11-23.

- Home: https://www.ixartpark.org/
- Rentals: https://www.ixartpark.org/rentals

Paraphrase of those two pages. Phone, email, street address, and EIN were not copied.

The pages say the nonprofit Ix Arts Foundation, a 501(c)(3), operates Ix Art Park and The Looking Glass immersive art museum. They call The Looking Glass Virginia's first immersive art museum and say it shows installations by more than 25 local and regional artists. They also mention a Rabbit Hole art lounge on the home page.

The outdoor park is described as a mural and sculpture area, open year-round to the public and free of charge, and as the site of events, festivals, workshops, and the Market Central Saturday farmers market. The pages locate Ix in the heart of Charlottesville, a few blocks from the downtown mall. They do not mention EcoDome, a 50-foot dome, or a geodesic dome.

The rentals page says The Looking Glass and the outdoor art park can be rented separately or together, including private events. An outdoor rental is described as including a stage, lighting, and picnic tables. It says the concrete platform that makes up most of the available outdoor space measures about 150 by 100 feet.

Neither page names EcoDome as a tenant, partner, or scheduled event.
