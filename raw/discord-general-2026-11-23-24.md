## November 23, morning

Around 9:14 AM, Owen told the channel about the OKF / Karpathy-style LLM wiki, a weekly librarian, the repository, and the knowledge graph. He offered to transfer ownership to a shared GitHub repository or organization. He said his bot would eventually want read-only Discord access so it could help curate.

Around 9:23 AM he posted the weekly update that became pull request #4. Reactions were positive. HoneyBeeT thanked him and said the group is exploring how to integrate with the wiki.

On display technology, Owen suggested projection as a starting point and OLED or LED panels as the ideal end state for immersion. He left open how well projection works on a sunny day. Cocolata8 replied that the plan is projection for the proof of concept and LED for the final build. That is recorded as the team's stated plan, not as a signed decision.

Owen also suggested that if the projection dome is portable, rentals for weddings and corporate events could help fund it.

On contribution, Owen described a process: agents can check the repository out locally; people can send him a GitHub handle for read and write access; the graph rebuilds when changes merge; he recommends recording and transcribing meetings into the catalog, in the pattern of an organizational second brain; and he offered a dedicated GitHub organization for EcoDome.
