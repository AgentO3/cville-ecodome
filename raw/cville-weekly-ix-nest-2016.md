# C-VILLE Weekly note on an IX Art Park exhibit

Accessed 2026-11-23. URL: https://c-ville.com/best-c-ville-2016-health-fitness/

The page is a "Best of C-VILLE 2016" roundup dated August 24, 2016. Almost all of it is unrelated to EcoDome. This capture keeps only the IX Art Park passage. Artist surnames and other people's quotations were not copied.

In a section on IX Art Park, the article describes an exhibit called Nest. It says the exhibit is a large nest and that a geodesic dome underneath it had been on the playa at Burning Man. The article does not use the name EcoDome, and it does not call the structure a vegetative dome or give its diameter.

This 2016 description is not evidence that the structure was still in the courtyard on 2026-11-23. The IX Art Park pages captured the same day do not mention it.
