# CanvasRebel interview with the EcoDome founder

Accessed 2026-11-23. URL: https://canvasrebel.com/meet-coco-hunter/

The page is a Stories & Insights conversation dated April 10, 2025. This capture paraphrases project claims only. It leaves out the founder's surname, another personal name given in the introduction, personal quotations, a photographer credit, and the contact block (a studio site and social accounts).

The founder is the person this wiki already calls Coco. The interview describes a long practice working with artists, educators, technologists, and entrepreneurs, and presents EcoDome as that person's project.

Project claims, as stated in the interview rather than as checked facts:

- EcoDome is described as Charlottesville's first fully immersive, multipurpose creative space.
- It features a 50-foot interactive dome for gatherings of up to 250 people.
- The interior is described as immersive media, concerts, storytelling, and educational programming, and as unlike what the region then offered.
- The problem it names is limited access to advanced digital literacy tools, creative technology, and innovative programming, for educators, students, artists, and residents.
- The approach joins technology, sustainability, wellness, and creativity. The space is described as an environmentally conscious model meant to show renewable energy, sustainable materials, and community-driven design.
- Values named in the interview: innovation, sustainability, wellness (including mindfulness), and inclusivity.
- As of that date, the founder described a community membership campaign, said it was backed by the City of Charlottesville, said the project was speaking with location partners and had strong support from the City, and aimed at a prime downtown location. The interview gives no street address and does not attach a contract, ordinance, or permit.
- Growth is described as grassroots, through in-person gatherings, with depth of connection preferred to a large headcount.
- Fred is named as lead designer and builder. A sentence attributed to Fred was not copied.
