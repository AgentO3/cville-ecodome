# What exists

The [community notes](../sources/community-context.md) say Owen prototyped spatial simulators from the EARS specification. They identify a Grok Bot version at <https://ecodome-simulator.netlify.app>, built with Three.js and iPhone AR Quick Look. They name Claude and Muse prototypes and do not record URLs for those builds.[^community-context]

A later note records the three links Owen shared so people can explore and prototype dome experiences without a full-size dome.[^prototype-links]

The explore URL is an entry on the same Grok Bot site the notes already named. The base URL remains <https://ecodome-simulator.netlify.app>. The note is summarized at [spatial-simulator prototype links](../sources/simulator-prototype-links.md).

The notes are the source for the claim that this URL implements the spec. The [specification](../sources/ears-spec.md) itself does not list a deployment URL. The product the spec describes is the [spatial simulator](../simulator/spatial-simulator.md). An [outdoor full-scale placement](outdoor-placement.md) is recorded separately.

The same notes mention Owen's earlier WebVR work, the Roomkey A-Frame art gallery at <https://agento3.github.io/a-frame-art-gallery/>. That gallery is prior work, not an EcoDome build.

The [IX Art Park digital twin](ix-art-park-twin.md) is a different site, credited to Kahlil.
