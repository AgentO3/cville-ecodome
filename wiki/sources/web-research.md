# How to read confidence

The report uses three labels, and the concept pages keep them:

- **Confirmed** means a fetched page, filing extract, event listing, or news article states it. A confirmed official-site sentence is still the project's own description of a venue that is not built.
- **Self-reported** means the claim appears in founder-authored material (the official site's marketing, LinkedIn, CanvasRebel, Alignable) without an independent document.
- **Unconfirmed** or **not found** means this pass looked and did not find a corroborating public page. Absence from search is not proof that no filing exists.
