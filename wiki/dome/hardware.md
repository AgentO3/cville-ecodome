# Available offer

The [feasibility research](../sources/feasibility-research.md) says a frame and cover are available to purchase, and that the offer does not include a projection liner or AV equipment. It does not name a seller, a price, a manufacturer, or a year. A fair used price, the study says, should sit well below new list prices, adjusted for age, cover wear, and whether the hardware is complete. If the cover is translucent rather than blackout, the study says to budget a blackout cover or an interior liner.[^feasibility]

That offer is not the same claim as the Pacific Domes package proposed on the [geometry page](geometry.md), and it is not a purchase.

# Typical 50-foot specifications

The study collects published figures for domes of this size. Capacity, it says, is set by the local fire official. A projection layout with reclined seats, a stage, and an AV position holds fewer people than a brochure.

The [geometry page](geometry.md) keeps the project's own 175 seated and 250 standing figures beside these brochure ranges. The 25-foot apex matches the specification's hemisphere height in feet. The floor area is new to the wiki.

# The 50-foot brochure itself

The [Pacific Domes brochure](../sources/pacific-domes-50ft-brochure.md) retrieved 2026-11-25 is a primary sheet for several figures the study had quoted from a "Pacific Domes spec sheet." It prints weight 6,130 pounds (2,780.5 kg), 555 struts, frequency 6, and 100 mph wind with no snow. It also prints a snow line, "20 lbs @ 130 mph," without saying whether the 20 pounds is per square foot. The included frame is standard 1.3 inch galvanized steel. The package lists a vinyl cover, two round hoop doors, two HVAC ports, anchors for any surface, and an assembly manual. Seating on that sheet is 300 standing, 200 in rows, 170 at a buffet, and 150 at round tables. Ceiling height is 24.5 feet, which is the low end of the study's 24.5 to 25 foot apex range. Floor area is 1,962.5 square feet, at the top of the study's about 1,905 to 1,962 range.[^pacific-brochure]

The brochure's pallet lines print inches and meters that do not convert into each other. Neither unit was corrected here. The brochure prints no price. The August 2024 list below stays the study's quotation of a different price list. This sheet is not a purchase.
