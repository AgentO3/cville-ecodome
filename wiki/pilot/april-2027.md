# Proposal

The [AI Explorers deck](../sources/ai-explorers-deck.md) proposes a five-day EcoDome showcase with Tom Tom on April 21–25, 2027, branded "EcoDome × Tom Tom 2027." The temporary dome is called the first experiment. The pilot would help the team assess community demand and the requirements for a lasting venue. Launch remains subject to funding, site approval, and final agreements.[^explorers-deck]

The deck does not name the site. [IX Art Park](../venue/ix-art-park.md) comes from the community notes, not from these slides. The structure it proposes is the 50-foot package on the [geometry page](../dome/geometry.md). [Site options](../venue/site-options.md) compares every place claim now on file.

The [official site](../sources/official-site.md), read on 2026-11-23, does not mention April 2027, Tom Tom, or this five-day showcase. It describes a venue still "coming" to Charlottesville.[^official-site] The April 10, 2025 interview, which predates the deck, describes talks with location partners and an aim of a downtown location. It does not mention Tom Tom. The later deck still says the site must be confirmed, so the interview does not settle the pilot's site.[^interview]

# Path and readiness

The deck says the team is ready to build on four items: five programming concepts submitted; build, creative, and technical leads identified; a supplier estimate and technical proposals received; a working budget prepared for financial review.

The path it prints:

1. Confirm site and delivery responsibilities.
2. Secure sponsorship and finalize scope.
3. Assemble, integrate, and test.
4. Showcase at Tom Tom, April 21–25, 2027.

What it says still unlocks the pilot: site access and approvals; funding and supplier payment terms; projection, power, and staffing plans; final programming and partner agreements.

Slides of the installation are labeled concept visualizations, or an original dome photograph with simulated interior projection. Exterior visibility is to be tested. One aerial concept carries an Oesch Environmental Design mark. A night photograph of exterior projection is credited to Pacific Domes.

# Programs named on the deck

- [In-dome experiences](../dome/immersive-content.md), including NEXTGEN youth experiences and operator-led sessions.
- A proposed pre-festival hackathon: "a media lab for a day," asking what Charlottesville makers, builders, tinkerers, and explorers can make together. Several of those slides carry a Reality Hack mark. The deck does not define that name beyond the mark and a caption that one visualization uses a Reality Hack photograph.
- The [image-to-experience prototype](../prototypes/image-to-experience.md), labeled work in progress.

The community notes mention a different hackathon idea, a WebXR toolkit around [Dome Tools](../tools/dome-tools.md). The deck does not mention Dome Tools. Both ideas are unresolved on the [questions page](../questions/open-questions.md).

# November 25 status from Coco

On 2026-11-25 Coco (Cocolata8) told Owen that the Tom Tom Foundation has sent a letter of support. That letter is received. It is not a site booking, a permit, or a signed show contract, and the letter is not filed here. The festival window she used is still April 21-25, 2027. She said the working plan budgets four days of public programming. The deck above proposes a five-day showcase. Exact show dates are still open. The paraphrase is the [tracker update](../sources/coco-tracker-update.md).[^coco-tracker]

She wants that featured first on the tracker. Dates are not set. The note does not say Hackadome is the deck's one-day media lab.

# Money and reporting

The fundraising goal, the approximate allocation, pay-what-you-can admission, and the proposed sponsor terms are on the [budget page](budget-and-sponsorship.md). Coco is named on the deck as founder and as the person to start a sponsor conversation with. This page does not repeat a surname.

Proposed reporting, still to be agreed, would cover programs and sessions delivered, session visits and supported places, optional feedback, and whether agreed sponsor benefits were delivered. The deck promises a short post-pilot summary, aggregate results only, unique-visitor counts only when they can be counted reliably, and no audience target or research outcome.
