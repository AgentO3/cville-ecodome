# EcoDome Charlottesville — context notes (from Owen, Nov 2026)

Project-level facts gathered from the community Discord (do NOT copy personal details or verbatim quotes from other members into the public repo; refer to people by first name/role only where needed):

- EcoDome is a proposed immersive dome venue in Charlottesville, VA, associated with IX Art Park. Proposed dome: 50 ft (15.24 m) diameter hemisphere, ~250 audience capacity, hemispherical video dome.
- Coco and Tina presented the dome at the Cville AI Explorers meetup (Nov 2026). Tina is an immersive installation / interactive sculpture artist.
- Existing digital twin by Kahlil: https://eco-dome-ix-art-park.netlify.app/ (360 views, satellite placement).
- Owen prototyped spatial simulators: Grok Bot version at https://ecodome-simulator.netlify.app (Three.js + iPhone AR Quick Look, built from the EARS spec attached as ecodome-spec.txt); also Claude and Muse prototypes. A full-size virtual dome was placed outdoors in AR (YouTube short: https://youtube.com/shorts/FAGsv3jeyNU).
- Related tools: Dome Tools by Prefrontal Cortex (Unity-based real-time dome projection, https://prefrontalcortex.de/en/projects/dome-tools/). Idea floated: a WebXR toolkit pulling in Dome Tools for a hackathon; virtual multiplayer watch parties.
- Owen's prior WebVR work: Roomkey A-Frame art gallery https://agento3.github.io/a-frame-art-gallery/.
- Values discussion: community prefers EcoDome stay independent of frontier AI labs / corporate funders to protect its experimental, human-agency-focused ethos; funding model is an open question.
- Lead: Kahlil's friend James owns a dome; Kahlil recently bought dome-related equipment. Open question: are there similar domes near Charlottesville to tour?
