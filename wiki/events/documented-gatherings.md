# What these events are

Each listing below was fetched for the [November 23, 2026 research report](/sources/web-research.md). None of them is an opening of a built dome. The [timeline](/timeline/public-record.md) is the date order. The [IX Art Park](/venue/ix-art-park.md) page separates this campus activity from a construction site.[^web-research]

# March 9, 2025 — unveiling gathering

**Confirmed.** Free "ECODOME EXCLUSIVE – New Location at Three Notch'd!" / "Metaverse & Site Unveiling," 2:30–3:30 p.m., at Three Notch'd Craft Kitchen & Brewery, 520 2nd Street SE. The listing says the gathering was relocated to Three Notch'd at IX and that attendees would hear where ECODOME will rise. Status on the page: completed. Organizer: Victoria Bryant Torres. The learn-more link pointed at `cocolatastudio.com/ecodome-cville`, which did not serve usable EcoDome content on the research date. [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/ecodome-exclusive-new-location-at-three-notchd-tickets-1245565553099).[^eventbrite-unveiling]

The copy talks about where the dome will rise. That makes the brewery the meeting place on this listing, not a demonstrated build site.

# May 3, 2025 — potluck in Afton

**Confirmed as a completed listing.** Free potluck, 4:00–10:00 p.m., in Afton, Virginia, organized by Victoria Bryant Torres. The description promised EcoDome updates. The street address from the listing is omitted here. [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/potluck-party-magic-celebrating-cocos-birthday-tickets-1333255746829).[^eventbrite-afton]

# April 14, 2026 — Cville Great Mother Fest

**Confirmed as a festival, not a dome opening.** IX Art Park, noon–7 p.m., free, tied to the Great Mother March. [NBC29](https://www.29news.com/2026/04/13/first-ever-cville-great-mother-fest-coming-charlottesville-tuesday/) (April 13) quotes Coco Hunter as founder of the festival and does not name EcoDome in the fetched text. [CBS19](https://www.cbs19news.com/news/the-great-mother-march-comes-to-charlottesville/article_2179d506-4a46-43fb-8c87-8f499bef4ca3.html) (April 14) identifies Coco Hunter as founder of ECODOME Charlottesville and a contributor to the march. A related GoFundMe, launched or dated March 2, 2026, said it was administered by "ECODOME Foundation" and later showed $765 raised of $2,200 from 9 donations. That is a festival fundraiser. The foundation name is unconfirmed as a nonprofit. See [media coverage](/media/coverage.md).[^nbc29][^cbs19]

# November 18, 2026 — Cville AI Explorers

**Confirmed as the public listing, not as a transcript.** Meeting #19, Show & Share, Studio IX, 969 2nd St SE, 6:00–8:00/8:30 p.m. The Meetup listing names Coco Hunter for a 15-minute EcoDome talk, described as an emerging immersive, interactive AI environment for education, AI literacy, community engagement, research, and sustainability. Tina is not on that speaker list. The same meeting is listed by [UVA Engineering](https://engineering.virginia.edu/news-events/events/cville-ai-explorers-cvaiex). No public transcript or press write-up of the talk was found. [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/cville-tech/events/317209160/).[^meetup-ai]

The [community notes](/community/plans.md) say Coco and Tina presented in November 2026. The listing and the notes disagree on who the public program named. The [AI Explorers deck](/sources/ai-explorers-deck.md) is the slide file associated with that setting in this wiki. This research pass could not open the Google Slides original.
