# What this page is

The [feasibility research](../sources/feasibility-research.md) summarizes Virginia tent and zoning rules as planning research. It is not a legal opinion, and no EcoDome permit application is on file. The [pilot budget](../pilot/budget-and-sponsorship.md) still shows about $2,000 for insurance and permits as a proposal.[^feasibility]

# A 50-foot dome needs a permit each time it goes up

The study says Virginia exempts only tents of 900 square feet or less with an occupant load of 50 or fewer. A 50-foot dome, about 1,960 square feet, always needs a building permit, every time it is erected. The local fire official may also require an operational permit. Albemarle County's tent guideline, as cited, says structures over 900 square feet or 50 occupants need a building permit from Community Development. Applications with 50 or more occupants must include a site and floor plan. There is no occupancy before final inspection, and the permittee must keep inspecting the structure while it stands.

# The 180-day line

Tents and membrane structures erected for less than 180 days are temporary and follow the fire code's tent chapter. Longer than 180 days, the structure is no longer a temporary tent and must meet the full Virginia Construction Code, including fire protection, egress, and accessibility. A year-round dome venue is therefore a larger regulatory job than a seasonal run. Separately, membrane structures over 7,500 square feet must meet the building code. A 50-foot dome is well under that area threshold. The [hybrid model](../business/hybrid-model.md) uses this line to prefer a seasonal venue plus mobile rentals.

# Fire-code items the study says to plan around

- At least two exits for 10 to 199 occupants, more above that. Each exit at least 72 inches wide, spaced around the perimeter, with no point more than 100 feet from an exit. Public aisles at least 44 inches. Exit signs and egress lighting while occupied.
- Cover, liner, drapes, and floor coverings must meet NFPA 701, or be approved flame-retardant treated. Virginia requires a permanently affixed label identifying size and fabric type.
- Smoking, open flames, fireworks within 100 feet, flammable-liquid-fueled equipment inside, and combustible materials such as hay or straw in assembly use are prohibited.
- Generators at least 20 feet away. Cooking and heating equipment at least 10 feet from exits. LP-gas containers outside. Combustible vegetation cleared 30 feet around.
- A state overview of tent rules lists motion pictures in tents as prohibited except for "safety film." The study says that language dates from flammable film stock, that digital projection is used in event domes elsewhere, and that written confirmation from the local fire official is still required before booking a show.

# Zoning

In Albemarle County, the study says weddings and other event-space rentals on Rural Areas land need a special use permit, except at bona fide farm wineries, breweries, and distilleries. A county guidance document put that permit at about $2,500 and the review at a minimum of six months. Events over 200 attendees at those farm sites still need a special use permit, and a summary of the rules cites 21 acres for agritourism events there. The practical implication in the study is to partner with an existing permitted venue rather than permitting land the project controls. Each city and county differs. How that sits with the still-unconfirmed EcoDome site is on [site options](../venue/site-options.md).

The study also notes a business license, sales-tax registration if taxable items are sold, commercial driver rules if the truck is heavy enough, a Virginia ABC license if alcohol is served, and ADA access for ticketed public shows. Those are listed as items to confirm, not as completed filings.
