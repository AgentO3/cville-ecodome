# Claims as printed

The [AI Explorers deck](/sources/ai-explorers-deck.md) uses three themes and one comparison. This page records the sentences on the slides. It does not retrieve or evaluate the cited papers.[^explorers-deck]

| Theme | Claim on the slide | Citation printed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wonder | Awe in planetariums is linked with greater interest in the subject. | Kanerva et al., 2024 |
| Connection | Awe research links looking beyond ourselves with greater collective engagement. | Bai et al., 2017 |
| Participation | Interactive museum research shows how design can support families learning together. | Price, Jewitt & Moussouri, 2022 |

A further slide states that in a study of 781 undergraduates learning about moons, the group that saw the lesson in a fulldome theater retained more than the group that saw the same lecture and visuals on a classroom flat screen. That slide does not print an author, a year, or a retention figure. It labels the surrounding scene a proposed experience visualization and credits NASA photographs. The image identifiers are small; they are not transcribed here.
