## Event dome covers

Outdoor fabrics:

- Event Lite: lightweight, described as perfect for digital projections. Logos and a full-cover print are offered. Translucent, so images can be seen inside and outside. 100 percent coated polyester, 7 oz per square yard. Flame resistant and water and mildew resistant. Many colors.
- Event Vinyl: described as durable for long-term outdoor events. Logos can be applied. Available in blackout for daytime projection or controlled lighting. Vinyl-coated polyester, 17 oz per square yard. Flame resistant and water, mildew, and UV resistant. Many colors. Blackout vinyl has limited color options.

Indoor fabrics:

- Stretch Lite: opalescent, for indoor events, described as fitting the frame closely and as perfect for digital projections. Lighter colors are translucent, so images can be seen inside and outside. Lycra blend, 5 oz per square yard. Flame resistant. Many colors.
- Theatre Fabric: for indoor theatres, described as satisfying interior daytime projection. Either 100 percent cotton or 100 percent polyester, both 18 oz per square yard. Flame resistant. Black only.

Projection day and night: Event Lite and Stretch Lite are described as ideal for projection and lighting. For nighttime projection, those translucent covers allow images inside and outside the dome. For daytime events that require interior projection, the brochure offers blackout vinyl and theatre fabric.

## Printed covers and branding

Custom printing uses dye sublimation onto the fabric for artwork, graphics, logos, and branding. Vinyl appliques for logos and graphics are described as temporary or event-specific, compatible with Event Lite and Event Vinyl, and removable. Powder coating of the frame is an optional colored finish that the brochure says will not fade.

## Structure and options

The frame is described as free-standing, with no support poles, and strong enough to support lighting and sound equipment. Frames pack down for shipping. Instructions and optional on-site training are offered. Anchors are supplied for a deck, asphalt, concrete, or earth. Tubing size is described as something the manufacturer will set with the buyer's music, lighting, and interior crews. The included frame is the standard 1.3 inch size above.

Optional features:

- Bay windows, skylight roofs, and small round portal windows that can be removed or replaced with screens
- Standard round hoop door with a reinforcement hoop; openings for single or double pre-hung doors are also offered
- Flooring, and deck plans for permanent installations
- Connecting two or more domes through the round doors with short or long tunnels
- Heating or air-conditioning, HVAC openings, and optional solar fans. A removable roof may be left open, and base flaps may be rolled up
