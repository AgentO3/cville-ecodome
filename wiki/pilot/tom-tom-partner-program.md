# Community Partner Program

The [program page](https://tomtomfoundation.org/partner-program), as recorded with the Discord note, says there is no fee. Partners bring materials or a venue. Sponsors of $2,500 or more are to be reported by March 1. A LinkedIn post said Round 2 closes November 28. That deadline was not confirmed on the program page when the note was written. A virtual interest meeting is December 8 at noon Eastern Time.

This page does not say EcoDome has joined the program. The [Discord notes](../community/discord-nov-23-24.md) are the channel discussion that pointed at these pages.

On November 25, Coco said the Tom Tom Foundation has sent a letter of support, and that tracker items which had been marked not started now have conversations underway. The letter is the one item she described as received. It is not membership in this partner program, and it is not a booking of the April 21-25 window. She is budgeting four days of public programming inside that window. The [April 2027 pilot](april-2027.md) still carries the deck's five-day proposal beside that. The paraphrase is the [tracker update](../sources/coco-tracker-update.md).[^coco-tracker]
