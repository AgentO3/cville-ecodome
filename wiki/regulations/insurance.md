# Coverages named in the study

The [feasibility research](../sources/feasibility-research.md) is not an insurance quote. It lists policies a mobile dome business is commonly asked to carry.[^feasibility]

| Coverage | Why the study includes it |
| --- | --- |
| Commercial general liability | Venues and municipalities commonly ask for $1 million per occurrence and $2 million aggregate, plus a certificate naming them as additional insured |
| Inland marine | The dome and AV gear in storage, in transit, and on site. A standard property policy often does not cover that. Insure at replacement value and ask about wind and theft while installed |
| Commercial auto | Truck and trailer, plus hired and non-owned auto |
| Workers' compensation | Required in Virginia once a business regularly employs more than two people. Part-time counts, and subcontractors' employees count toward that total |
| Umbrella | Often $1 million to $5 million. Some clients require it |
| Liquor liability | If alcohol is served at the operator's own ticketed events. Often $1 million per occurrence |
| Event cancellation | Optional protection for ticket revenue |
| Professional liability | Optional if the business designs content or produces events |

The study says many general-liability policies limit amusement devices, aerial work, or temporary structures, so the policy has to say it covers erecting and dismantling a geodesic structure. It also says institutional clients expect certificates. UVA, it notes, keeps pre-certified tent vendors on file for stage permits.

**Budget estimate only:** $12,000 to $25,000 a year for a combined package in the first year, to be replaced with quotes. Premiums depend on revenue, payroll, claims, and limits. The [pilot budget](../pilot/budget-and-sponsorship.md) instead allocates about $2,000 for insurance and permits together. Those numbers are not reconciled. The crew that triggers workers' compensation is described on [logistics](../operations/logistics-and-crew.md).
