# Summary

EcoDome is a proposed portable immersive venue in Charlottesville, Virginia, where residents could experience art, explore emerging technology, and create together. The [AI Explorers deck](/sources/ai-explorers-deck.md) describes a temporary dome as the first experiment. An April 2027 pilot would be used to judge community demand and the requirements for a lasting venue.[^explorers-deck]

The [community notes](/sources/community-context.md) describe the same effort as a hemispherical video dome associated with [IX Art Park](/venue/ix-art-park.md), about 50 feet across, with an audience on the order of 250 people.[^community-context] The deck's nominal size is also 50 feet. It distinguishes a proposed seated capacity of 175 from a proposed standing capacity of 250, and it says the site is not yet confirmed. See [dome geometry](/dome/geometry.md) and the [April 2027 pilot](/pilot/april-2027.md).

A separate [spatial simulator](/simulator/spatial-simulator.md) is specified so people can explore, place, and preview a digital model before the architecture is final.[^ears-spec]

# What the public web showed on November 23, 2026

A [public-web pass](/sources/web-research.md) that day found no operating dome under this name. The official site, [ecodomecville.com](https://ecodomecville.com/), is live and frames the project as "COMING TO CVILLE": a 50-foot LED-panel edutainment venue for about 250 people. That wording is **confirmed as the site's own description**. It is not independent evidence that a building exists.[^web-research]

The same pass found a Virginia LLC, [ECODOME CVILLE, LLC](/organization/ecodome-cville-llc.md), incorporated February 21, 2025. It did not find a city permit, a locked site, or a 501(c)(3) record. The dated record is the [timeline](/timeline/public-record.md). Gatherings are listed under [events](/events/documented-gatherings.md). News is on the [media page](/media/coverage.md).
