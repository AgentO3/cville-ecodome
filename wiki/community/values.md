# Preference recorded in the notes

The November 2026 [community notes](../sources/community-context.md) say the community prefers that EcoDome stay independent of frontier AI labs and corporate funders, in order to protect an experimental, human-agency-focused ethos. They call the funding model an open question. The notes do not present that preference as a bylaw or a vote.[^community-context]

# Proposal in the deck

The [AI Explorers deck](../sources/ai-explorers-deck.md) asks sponsors to help fund a $175,000 pilot. Support can be cash, equipment, or services. Proposed recognition includes a name or logo on an in-dome thank-you screen. The terms are collected on the [budget page](../pilot/budget-and-sponsorship.md). The deck does not say whether a sponsor would be a frontier lab or a corporate funder, and it does not discuss the independence preference.[^explorers-deck]

# Other public funding language

These are separate pages, not a single plan.

- The [official site](../sources/official-site.md), read on 2026-11-23, invites people to become a founding member, donate, or spread the word. It does not print the $175,000 figure.[^official-site]
- The April 10, 2025 interview describes a community membership campaign as the way the project was then raising money, and describes that campaign as backed by the City of Charlottesville. That is the founder's account at that date, not a filed grant or ordinance.[^interview]
- The [Alignable listing](../sources/alignable-listing.md) lists founding circle memberships, investment and membership opportunities, corporate sponsorships, and renewable-energy partnerships among 23 offerings. It does not give amounts.[^alignable]

The [organization page](../organization/ecodome-cville-llc.md) names Ecodome Cville, LLC and Ecodome Foundation. It does not say which of them would receive a donation or a sponsorship.

All of these statements stand. The [open questions](../questions/open-questions.md) page keeps the unresolved relationship among them. The [April 2027 pilot](../pilot/april-2027.md) is the event the deck is raising money for. The [community plans](plans.md) are the cadence around the discussion, not a funding decision.

The same public pages describe sustainability as part of the project: renewable energy, sustainable materials, and eco-conscious practice on the site and in the interview, and a "sustainability" sponsor focus area on the deck. None of them is an energy design or a measured footprint.
