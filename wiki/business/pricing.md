# Published benchmarks

The [feasibility research](../sources/feasibility-research.md) quotes other operators. These are their published figures, not EcoDome prices.[^feasibility]

| Provider | What the study records |
| --- | --- |
| SoFlo Studio, Miami, 50-foot | Dome $8,000 a day; install and strike $12,000; projection $10,000 for 180 degrees or $25,000 for 360 degrees; ballast $2,400 a week; permits from $6,500 in Miami |
| Pacific Domes, 50-foot | $4,300 a day for Eventlite, or $7,000 a day for vinyl or blackout; setup and strike $16,500 for four staff and a 10,000-pound reach forklift; projection quoted separately; shipping, travel, and equipment extra |
| Science Museum of Virginia | Fixed 250-seat dome theater from about $2,500, operator included |

The study says a one-day Miami booking of a 50-foot dome with 360-degree projection would be well over $45,000 once install, ballast, and permits are included, and that bringing an out-of-state supplier to Virginia would add freight and crew travel.

# Suggested central Virginia prices

The study labels the next table as estimates.

| Package | Suggested price | What it includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dome shell | $7,500 to $12,000 per event, up to three days on site | Dome, install and strike, ballast or anchoring, basic lighting, permit handling |
| Dome plus 360-degree projection | $18,000 to $30,000 per event | Shell plus liner, projection, audio, operator, and a licensed content loop |
| Custom content | Quoted per project, often $5,000 or more | Branded or bespoke fulldome visuals |
| Add-ons | Cost plus 15 to 25 percent | HVAC, generator, flooring, furniture, extra days, rented from local partners |
| Ticketed shows | $15 to $30 per adult | Compared with about $8 at a college dome, $13 to $15 at science-museum domes, a $15 add-on at the Dalí Museum, and $29.95 at an immersive dome on Catalina Island |

The study suggests a deposit, commonly 50 percent, plus a weather and cancellation policy. The [April 2027 pilot](../pilot/budget-and-sponsorship.md) instead describes free and pay-what-you-can admission. Those are different products: a rental price list and a sponsored pilot. Neither has been adopted. How the estimates behave in a year of bookings is the [profit model](financial-model.md). Who else sets the local price is [competition](../competition/central-virginia.md).
