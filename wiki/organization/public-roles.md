# EcoDome

**Coco Hunter, founder. Confirmed as a public identification.** The [official site](https://ecodomecville.com/) describes Coco Hunter as ECODOME founder and visionary. [CBS19](https://www.cbs19news.com/news/the-great-mother-march-comes-to-charlottesville/article_2179d506-4a46-43fb-8c87-8f499bef4ca3.html) (April 14, 2026) identifies Coco Hunter as "the Founder of ECODOME Charlottesville" in a story about the Great Mother March. The [November 18, 2026 Meetup listing](/events/documented-gatherings.md) names Coco Hunter as the EcoDome speaker. LinkedIn (profile `cocolata-ecodome`) says "Founder at ECODOME Cville"; the About text used here came from a search index because the profile fetch timed out, so treat the longer About claims as **self-reported**.[^official-site][^cbs19]

A UVA Contemplative Sciences Center staff page lists Coco Hunter as Community Programs Coordinator. **That page does not mention EcoDome.**[^web-research]

**Tina. Not found on the public web as an EcoDome role.** A portfolio at [justbeing.space/Portfolio](https://www.justbeing.space/Portfolio) describes immersive installation work in Virginia. This research found no public page that names Tina as an EcoDome presenter, founder, or team member, and the November 18 Meetup speaker list does not include Tina. The [community notes](/sources/community-context.md) say Coco and Tina presented at the Cville AI Explorers meetup, and the [pilot deck](/dome/immersive-content.md) credits creative direction to Tina. Both accounts stay. They are not the same kind of source.[^web-research][^community-context]

**Akshita Tiwari. EcoDome role is self-reported.** Virginia Business (November 29, 2025) and UVA Engineering profile Akshita Tiwari as a UVA computer science student and co-founder of qMe and Proco. **Those articles do not mention EcoDome.** A LinkedIn post by Coco Hunter calls Akshita Tiwari a key part of the EcoDome team and founder of qMe. That is a founder-authored description.[^web-research]

**Fred, lead designer and builder. No last name, firm, or independent confirmation was found. Another local designer shares that first name. No public page links that person to EcoDome, so this wiki does not connect them.[^canvasrebel]

**Victoria Bryant Torres, event organizer. Confirmed for two listings.** Victoria Bryant Torres is the Eventbrite organizer of the March 9, 2025 unveiling and the May 3, 2025 potluck. Other public pages associate that name with White Lotus Eco Spa Retreat. Those pages do not by themselves define an EcoDome staff role.[^web-research]

**Kahlil Calavas. Association with the twin is inferential.** [The Futurist Network](https://thefuturist.network/about) lists Kahlil Calavas as creative technologist and CTO at TerraLux. The [digital twin](/prototypes/ix-art-park-twin.md) is branded with The Futurist Network and does not print his name. The [community notes](/sources/community-context.md) credit a twin to Kahlil. The research calls the link strong but inferential.[^futurist][^community-context]

# Different projects that share a name

Do not merge these into EcoDome Charlottesville. They turned up in the same searches:[^web-research]

- [Ekodome](https://ekodome.com/) sells geodesic-dome kits.
- [Eco-Dome](https://www.eco-dome.eu/) sells European dome-home kits.
- Cal-Earth's SuperAdobe "Eco-Dome" is an earthbag shelter type.
- A LinkedIn company page at `linkedin.com/company/ecodome` looked like a different company.
- "The Dome" in Virginia Beach, Coco's Adventure Factory in Charlottesville, the 2014 IX Art Park Kickstarter, and Charlottesville Ecovillage stories are different projects.
